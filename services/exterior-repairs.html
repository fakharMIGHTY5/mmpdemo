Exterior Repairs & Restoration

On a Philadelphia row property, most interior water damage arrives through the roof, the party wall or the parapet. Restoration that skips those is temporary.

Discuss this project 1-833-466-4788

What this actually is

Exterior repair is the step that makes everything else last. We do it first, and we do not begin interior restoration until the building is closed up, because finishing a ceiling under a roof that still leaks is money set on fire.

On row properties the usual suspects are the roof membrane or coating, the parapet and coping, party wall flashing, and penetrations that were sealed with caulk instead of flashed properly. On detached and stucco-clad buildings it is more often the window and trim detailing.

We scope the exterior work off the same assessment that found the leak, so the repair addresses the entry point we documented rather than a general list of things that could be improved.

Signs you have this problem

  • A roof deck or coating that is past its service life
  • Open mortar joints, spalled brick or a leaning parapet
  • Flashing that has lifted at a chimney, wall or skylight
  • A shared party wall with a neighbour where the water could come from either side
  • Ceiling stains on the top floor after wind-driven rain
  • Coping stones that are loose, cracked or missing

How the work runs

Every job in this category follows the same order.

  • 01

    Survey the envelope

    Roof, parapet, coping, flashing, pointing and penetrations. On foot, and by drone where the elevation requires it.

  • 02

    Prioritise

    What is letting water in now, what will in a year, and what can reasonably wait. Costed separately so you can choose.

  • 03

    Close the building

    Membrane, coating, flashing, pointing, parapet and coping work. The repairs that stop the water.

  • 04

    Verify

    Water testing where the entry point was ambiguous, so we know it is closed rather than hoping.

  • 05

    Restore inside

    Interior work begins once the exterior is proven, not before.

What is included
