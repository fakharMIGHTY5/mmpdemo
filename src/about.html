The founder

The habit that built the company.

We find the cause before quoting the repair. It sounds obvious. Almost nobody does it, because it takes equipment, training, and time before there is any money on the table.

Clients keep saying the same thing: Josh did not try to sell us. He focused on diagnosing the real problem.

Today, Moisture Master Pros serves Greater Philadelphia and Northern Delaware. MMP handles leak detection, mold remediation, water damage, drainage issues, foundation repair, air sealing and stucco repair. Florida is next.

When you call, a real human being answers the phone. We listen and respond quickly.

What we do not do

Four promises, written down so you can hold us to them.

  • We will not remediate mold without finding and fixing the source of the water. The mold would come back, and you would be right to be angry about it.
  • We will not restore an interior under an open envelope, where the property is still exposed to the elements. The outside gets closed first, every time.
  • We will not analyze the air samples we take. They go to an independent accredited laboratory.
  • We will not quote a repair we have not diagnosed. If we do not know the cause yet, we will say so. Rest assured we will find it.
