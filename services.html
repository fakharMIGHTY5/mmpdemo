How to choose

Not sure which one you have?

That is our job, not yours. A ceiling stain could be the roof, the party wall, a supply line or condensation, and those repairs cost very different amounts.

Common starting points

  • A stain that keeps coming back. Start with leak detection.
  • A basement that floods every storm. Start with drainage.
  • A musty smell you cannot place. Start with mold and air testing.
  • A crack you have started watching. Start with foundation repair.
  • Growth that came back after remediation. Start with air sealing.
  • A stucco home you have never had probed. Start with stucco remediation.
