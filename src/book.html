Before you book

The questions people ask at this point.

Because it is a different thing. A free estimate is a salesperson looking at your wall for twenty minutes so they can quote you a job. A paid assessment is a technician with thermal imaging and moisture meters producing a document with photographs, findings and a priced scope. You own it, and you can take it to an insurer, an engineer or a competitor. If you hire us for the repair, the fee comes off the invoice.

A video consultation is our eyes on your camera. It is fast, it is available anywhere in the US, and it is very good at telling you whether you have a small problem or a large one. It cannot take moisture readings or see inside a wall. An inspection is a technician on site with equipment, and it is what a repair gets scoped from.

Same day where we can. If water is actively moving through your building, call rather than filling in this form. The phone gets a person. The form gets a callback.

A video consultation, yes, anywhere in the US. We will also tell you what to ask a local contractor and what answers should worry you, which is worth having before you spend money with someone we cannot vouch for.

We document to the standard adjusters expect: moisture logs, photographs, category and class. That documentation is where homeowners most often lose money. We will not tell you what your policy covers, because that is between you and your carrier, but we will give you the evidence to ask properly.
