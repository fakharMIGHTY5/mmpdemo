Thermal

What the camera sees that you cannot.

A thermal camera does not see moisture. It sees temperature. Wet material changes temperature at a different rate, and that shows through paint, plaster and stucco long before a stain does.

It is also why thermal alone is not enough. A cold spot could be a draught or a pipe run. That is what the moisture meter is for.
