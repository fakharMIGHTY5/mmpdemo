Interior Repairs & Restoration

Most of our work ends with a hole in something. Drywall, plaster, trim, paint and flooring go back so that the only evidence left is the report.

Discuss this project 1-833-466-4788

What this actually is

We finish what we open. On most jobs the restoration is quoted at the same time as the repair so you know the whole number before anything starts, rather than finding out afterwards that putting the room back is someone else's problem.

Winter is usually the best time for this work. The exterior season is closed, our crews are available, and nothing about interior restoration depends on the weather.

Signs you have this problem

  • A room that has been opened up for a leak or a remediation
  • Water damaged drywall, plaster or trim
  • A ceiling that has been cut and never closed
  • Staining that keeps coming back through paint
  • Work left unfinished by someone else

How the work runs

Every job in this category follows the same order.

  • 01

    Scope the make good

    What was opened, what was damaged, and what it takes to put back.

  • 02

    Confirm it is dry

    Readings before anything is closed up, because closing up wet material is how this comes back.

  • 03

    Rebuild

    Framing, insulation, board, plaster, trim.

  • 04

    Finish

    Paint and flooring to match what was there.

What is included

  • Readings to prove the area is dry before closing
  • Framing and insulation replaced where it was damaged
  • Board, plaster, trim and paint
  • Flooring made good or replaced
  • Quoted alongside the repair so you see the whole number
