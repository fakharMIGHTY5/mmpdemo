The calls we get,

Some of these are a morning. Some are a week with an excavator in the garden. Either way you get told which one it is before anything starts.

  1. 01 Mold Remediation Contain, remove, treat and verify, with the moisture source fixed. Remediation without that is just an expensive delay. Major project
  2. 02 Fiberglass Rooftop Deck Leak Repairs Roof decks started going on Philadelphia homes about twenty years ago. The houses have settled since. The decks have not. Major project
  3. 03 Foundation Repairs Cracks, movement, failing parging and chronically wet walls. Repaired, then waterproofed so the repair holds. Major project
  4. 04 Leak Detection & Water Intrusion Investigations Find the actual source of the water before anyone opens a wall. Thermal imaging, moisture meters and drone survey, inside and out. Diagnostic
  5. 05 Air Sealing & Insulation Stop the air leakage that carries moisture into wall and roof cavities. It is the quiet cause behind a great deal of recurring mold. Major project
  6. 06 Stucco Remediation Failed stucco traps water against the sheathing and rots the frame behind it. Full-wall remediation with the drainage plane rebuilt correctly. Major project
  7. 07 Basement Waterproofing Keeping water out of a basement is a drainage problem before it is a sealing problem. Paint on the wall is not waterproofing. Major project
  8. 08 Water Damage Restoration Extraction, structural drying, removal of what cannot be saved, and rebuild. Documented to a standard an insurer will accept. Major project
