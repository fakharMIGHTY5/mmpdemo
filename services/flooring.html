Flooring Installation

We lift floors to get at subfloors, and we put floors back. On a water damaged room, laying a new floor over a substrate that is still wet is the most common way a job fails.

Discuss this project 1-833-466-4788

What this actually is

The substrate decides everything. We take readings before anything is laid, and we will not lay over a subfloor that has not come down to an acceptable moisture content, even if that means waiting.

Where the subfloor or the framing below has gone, that gets replaced first and it is in the scope before we start.

Signs you have this problem

  • Flooring lifted or cut during a leak investigation
  • Buckling, cupping or lifting boards
  • A floor that was laid over a damp substrate
  • Staining or soft spots underfoot
  • A room being put back after remediation

How the work runs

Every job in this category follows the same order.

  • 01

    Substrate readings

    Moisture content measured and recorded before anything is specified.

  • 02

    Repair below

    Subfloor and framing replaced where the readings or the damage require it.

  • 03

    Prepare

    Levelling and any membrane the substrate calls for.

  • 04

    Lay

    Installed to the manufacturer's requirements, with the readings on file.

What is included

  • Moisture readings on the substrate before laying
  • Subfloor and framing repair where it is needed
  • Levelling and membrane where the substrate requires it
  • Installation to the manufacturer's requirements
  • The readings kept on file in case of a later claim
