Foundation Repairs

A foundation crack is either cosmetic or structural, and the whole cost of the job depends on which one you have. That is the first thing we establish.

Discuss this project 1-833-466-4788

What this actually is

Not every crack is a structural problem. Shrinkage cracks in poured concrete are common and often cosmetic. Horizontal cracking, bowing, stair-step patterns through masonry and any crack that is actively widening are a different conversation.

We measure and document the movement, establish whether water is driving it, and repair accordingly. Crack injection, carbon fibre reinforcement, repointing, rebuilt parging, or underpinning where the footing itself has failed.

Then we waterproof, because almost every foundation problem in this region is a water problem wearing a structural hat. Repairing the wall without fixing the drainage that damaged it is how people end up paying twice.

Signs you have this problem

  • A crack that has visibly widened over a season
  • Stair-step cracking through brick or block mortar joints
  • Doors or windows that have started to stick on one side of the house
  • A wall that bows inward, or a floor that slopes
  • Chronic damp or efflorescence across a foundation wall
  • Crumbling parging or mortar at or below grade

How the work runs

Every job in this category follows the same order.

  • 01

    Assess and measure

    Crack mapping, movement measurement, moisture readings and a look at what the drainage around the building is doing.

  • 02

    Establish the cause

    Hydrostatic pressure, poor drainage, settlement or thermal movement. The repair depends entirely on which.

  • 03

    Structural repair

    Injection, carbon fibre, repointing, rebuilt parging or underpinning, matched to what we actually found.

  • 04

    Waterproof

    Membrane, drainage board and exterior water management so the repaired wall stays dry.

  • 05

    Monitor

    Documented readings so movement can be checked against a baseline rather than a memory.

What is included

  • Crack mapping and movement documentation
  • Structural assessment report
  • Injection, carbon fibre or masonry repair as required
  • Parging and repointing
  • Exterior waterproof membrane and drainage board
  • Coordination with a structural engineer where the scope calls for one
  • Workmanship guarantee
