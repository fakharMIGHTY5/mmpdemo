What you get

The report is the product.

Photographs annotated with what we found and where, moisture readings, the cause, and a priced scope of work.

It is written so somebody who is not us can read it: an insurer, an engineer, or another contractor.

  • Annotated photographs of every finding
  • Moisture readings with locations
  • The established cause, not a list of maybes
  • Priced scope, line by line

The equipment

Four instruments, one answer.

01

Thermal imaging

Wet material holds temperature differently. Thermal shows it through a finished surface.

02

Moisture meters

Thermal says where to look. A meter gives the actual moisture content.

03

Drone survey

Parapets, coping and roof decks photographed properly instead of guessed at.

04

Invasive probing

On stucco, readings taken through the wall are the only honest way to know.
