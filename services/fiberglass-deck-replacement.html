Fiberglass Deck Replacement

There is a point where patching a fiberglass deck stops being economical. We will tell you when you have reached it, and we will tell you when you have not.

Discuss this project 1-833-466-4788

What this actually is

A replacement is a chance to correct what was wrong the first time. Most of the decks we take up were laid flat, without an adequate fall to the outlet, and with the parapet detail finished after the deck rather than tied into it. We correct the fall, rebuild the substrate where it has gone, and detail the junctions so the system is continuous.

We survey, design the detail, and give you a priced scope before anything is lifted. On a job this size you should know what you are buying.

Signs you have this problem

  • Repairs that have already been done twice and failed twice
  • Water getting in at more than one junction
  • Soft spots underfoot, which means the substrate has gone
  • A deck that was laid without a fall, so water sits on it
  • Visible delamination across the field

How the work runs

Every job in this category follows the same order.

  • 01

    Survey and readings

    What is wrong, how far the damage goes into the structure, and what is salvageable.

  • 02

    Scope and price

    A written scope with the detail drawings for the junctions, priced, yours to keep.

  • 03

    Strip and rebuild

    Lift the old system, repair the substrate, correct the fall.

  • 04

    Lay and test

    New system laid continuous through the junctions, then wet tested.

What is included

  • A full survey of the substrate before any price is given
  • Correction of the fall to the outlet
  • Parapet and threshold details tied into the new system
  • Repair of water damaged framing found underneath
  • A wet test and a photo record of the build up
