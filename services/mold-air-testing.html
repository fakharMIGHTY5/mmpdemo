Mold & Air Quality Testing

Testing is worth paying for when a decision depends on the number: a transaction, a dispute, a health question. Otherwise, find the water.

Book a paid inspection 1-833-466-4788

What this actually is

We take air samples indoors and a matched outdoor control, plus surface or tape lift samples where there is something visible to sample, and send them to an independent accredited laboratory.

The outdoor control is what makes the indoor result mean anything. Spores exist everywhere; what matters is whether the indoor count and species profile differ meaningfully from the air outside the building on the same day.

You get the laboratory report and our interpretation of what it means in plain language. If it points to a problem, we scope the moisture repair that caused it. If it points to nothing, we tell you that too.

Signs you have this problem

  • A health concern that a doctor has asked you to investigate
  • A purchase or sale that needs a documented air quality result
  • A dispute with a landlord, builder, insurer or seller
  • Growth you can smell but cannot see
  • Verification needed after a remediation somebody else performed
  • A tenant complaint that needs answering with evidence

How the work runs

Every job in this category follows the same order.

  • 01

    Walk the property

    Visual survey, moisture readings and a conversation about what you are experiencing and where.

  • 02

    Sample

    Indoor air samples, a matched outdoor control, and surface or tape lifts where there is visible growth.

  • 03

    Laboratory analysis

    Independent accredited laboratory. We do not analyse our own samples.

  • 04

    Report and interpret

    The laboratory result, plus what it actually means for your building, in language you can act on.

What is included

  • Indoor air sampling with outdoor control
  • Surface and tape lift sampling where indicated
  • Independent accredited laboratory analysis
  • Written interpretation in plain language
  • Remediation scope if one is warranted
