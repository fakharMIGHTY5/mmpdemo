The founder

The habit that built the company.

Find the cause before quoting the repair. It sounds obvious. Almost nobody does it, because it takes equipment, training, and two hours before there is any money on the table.

Clients kept saying the same thing: he did not try to sell up. That is what happens when the diagnosis comes first.

That one habit grew into crews across Greater Philadelphia and northern Delaware, handling leak detection, mold remediation, water damage, drainage, foundations, air sealing and stucco. And an office team that answers the phone. Florida is next.

What we will not do

Four promises, written down so you can hold us to them.

  • We will not remediate mold without fixing the water. It would come back, and you would be right to be angry about it.
  • We will not restore an interior under an open envelope. The outside gets closed first, every time.
  • We will not analyse our own air samples. They go to an independent accredited laboratory.
  • We will not quote a repair we have not diagnosed. If we do not know the cause yet, we will say so.
