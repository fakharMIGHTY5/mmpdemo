Water Damage Restoration

Drying a building is a measured process with an end point, not a case of leaving fans running until it smells better.

Discuss this project 1-833-466-4788

What this actually is

Water that sits for more than a day or two stops being a water problem and becomes a microbial one. The clock matters more than almost anything else, which is why the first call should happen before the first estimate.

We extract standing water, then dry the structure itself. Framing, subfloor and wall cavities, with equipment placed against measured moisture readings rather than guesswork. We keep taking readings until the materials are back to a documented dry standard.

What cannot be dried gets removed under containment so the demolition does not spread contamination through the rest of the house. Then we rebuild what we took out. Every stage is photographed and logged, because that record is what makes an insurance claim go smoothly.

Signs you have this problem

  • A burst pipe, failed appliance or overflow in the last few days
  • Flooring that has cupped, buckled or lifted
  • A ceiling that is sagging or stained after a leak upstairs
  • Carpet or pad that has been wet for more than 24 hours
  • A smell that got worse rather than better after it dried on the surface
  • An insurance claim that needs documenting properly

How the work runs

Every job in this category follows the same order.

  • 01

    Stop the source

    There is no point drying a building that is still taking on water. Source first, always.

  • 02

    Extract and assess

    Standing water out, moisture mapped across every affected material, category and class established.

  • 03

    Structural drying

    Air movers and dehumidification placed against the readings, monitored daily, adjusted as the building dries.

  • 04

    Controlled removal

    Unsalvageable material removed under containment with negative air, so demolition does not spread the problem.

  • 05

    Rebuild

    Back to pre-loss condition, with the moisture log and photo record handed over for your claim.

What is included

  • Emergency water extraction
  • Moisture mapping and daily monitoring logs
  • Commercial drying and dehumidification
  • Containment and negative air during removal
  • Antimicrobial treatment where indicated
  • Reconstruction to pre-loss condition
  • Photo and moisture documentation for insurers
